---

A shared understanding of the work, concept, plan or idea takes the form of a concept map.

## The goal

Have no assumptions that have not been verified together such that the concept map is complete.

## How to Start

Add a node to the concept map that represents the topic with a label. Proceed in iterations.

## Each iteration

Add to the shared understanding via questions: 

```
❓ **<node label>**: <question>

➡️ <your recommended answer>
```

Answers to questions will form new nodes and edges in the concept map, which in turn may raise further unknowns in
the concept map.

## When to stop

When there are no further nodes to add to the concept map.
